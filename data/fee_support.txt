Document: Fee Management and Receipt Support
Document ID: FEE-001

Issue 1: Where can a student find a fee payment receipt?

Resolution:
1. Log in to the ERP portal using your authorized account.
2. Open the Fees or Fee Management section.
3. Look for Payment History or Fee Receipts.
4. Select the relevant payment to view the receipt.
5. If the receipt is unavailable, contact the authorized support team.

Issue 2: Payment successful, but receipt not generated.

Troubleshooting steps:
1. Verify the transaction status in authorized payment records.
2. Check whether the ERP system received the payment update.
3. Verify whether receipt generation is pending or failed.
4. Check application logs if authorized.
5. If unresolved, create a support ticket for investigation.

Safety rules:
- Never claim a payment succeeded without verification.
- Never ask users for passwords or OTPs.
- Never claim a refund or payment correction is complete unless verified.
- The instructions above are general guidance, not confirmation of an actual student's payment or receipt.
