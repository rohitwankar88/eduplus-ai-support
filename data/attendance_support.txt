Document: Attendance Troubleshooting
Document ID: ATT-001

Issue: Attendance record is missing or incorrect.

Troubleshooting steps:
1. Confirm the student ID and relevant date.
2. Check whether attendance was submitted by the authorized staff member.
3. Verify whether the record is pending synchronization.
4. Collect relevant details without requesting unnecessary personal data.
5. Escalate corrections to authorized staff.

Safety rules:
- Never modify attendance without authorization.
- This is fictional documentation for a portfolio project.

Issue: How to view attendance percentage and history.

Troubleshooting steps:
1. Log in to the ERP portal using your authorized student account.
2. Open the Attendance section from the dashboard.
3. Select the relevant semester or date range, if available.
4. Review the attendance percentage and subject-wise attendance history.
5. If the information is missing or incorrect, contact authorized support staff.

Note:
Menu names may vary depending on the ERP configuration.
The chatbot cannot access or verify live student records.
