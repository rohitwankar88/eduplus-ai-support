Document: Exam Portal Troubleshooting
Document ID: EXAM-001

Issue: Student cannot view examination results.

Troubleshooting steps:
1. Verify the student is using the correct portal.
2. Check whether results have officially been published.
3. Verify the student's access permissions.
4. Check for portal errors or service outages.
5. Escalate unresolved issues to the authorized examination team.

Safety rules:
- Never invent or disclose unverified examination results.
- This is fictional documentation for a portfolio project.

Issue: Student wants to check the examination timetable.

Guidance:
1. Log in to the official ERP portal using your authorized account.
2. Look for the Examination, Academics, or Notices section.
3. Check for a published examination timetable or schedule.
4. If the timetable is unavailable, contact the authorized examination team.

Safety rules:
- Do not invent examination dates, times, or schedules.
- Only treat officially published timetables as confirmed.
